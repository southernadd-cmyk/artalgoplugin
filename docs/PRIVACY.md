# ALGO/ART · Composition — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 8 October 2026

**Service:** ALGO/ART · Composition, an optional ChatGPT plugin powered by the original ALGO/ART generator.

**Operator:** ALGO/ART. For questions, raise a public or private request through the project's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/southernadd-cmyk/artalgoplugin).

## Information processed

When you use the plugin, ChatGPT may send the subject of your requested artwork, the selected composition algorithm, and a composition seed to the ALGO/ART composition service. The service uses that information to render three preview compositions or one full-size PNG.

The server loads the public original ALGO/ART website in a headless browser to perform rendering. The original website does not receive your ChatGPT account credentials from this plugin.

## What is stored

The plugin's application code has no user-account database and does not intentionally save prompts, generated compositions, or a history of your ChatGPT conversations. The hosting provider (Render) may process normal operational information such as request timestamps, IP addresses, server diagnostics, or access logs according to its infrastructure settings and policies. ChatGPT/OpenAI separately processes conversations and tool outputs under its applicable policies.

We do not intentionally use your artwork subject to build advertising profiles or sell it.

## Third-party services

The plugin uses Render for hosting, GitHub for source code, and the ALGO/ART public renderer hosted on GitHub Pages. ChatGPT/OpenAI may display images and process your interaction with this plugin. Their separate terms and privacy notices apply.

## Sharing and retention

We do not provide users with a public gallery of their ChatGPT composition requests. Operational logs may be retained by hosting providers under their standard retention practices. We cannot delete copies stored by ChatGPT or hosting providers from within the plugin.

## Your choices

You can stop using or uninstall the plugin at any time. Do not enter confidential information into the artwork-subject field. For questions about the plugin's processing, use the project's GitHub Issues page.

## Changes

This policy may be revised when the plugin's features or hosting change. The version linked from the plugin listing is the current policy.
